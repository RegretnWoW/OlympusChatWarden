# Embedded quest references

Generated from **LibQuestDB / QuestDB v0.1.0**, by Pimptasty, under its MIT license (included as `QuestData-LICENSE.txt`). Original release: https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/questdb/files/9014639 . Exact archive hash, branch, locale and coverage are in `QuestData-manifest.json`.

Upstream attributes its classic reference facts to the CMaNGOS `classic-db` project (GPL-3.0; https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db), re-expressed by the upstream pipeline. Forever-specific rows come from upstream native-client observations at interface 16001 / build 69913. Blizzard owns the underlying Warcraft setting, names and game content. MIT software licensing does not transfer those rights.

DEV6 processes all 5,020 known quests in this source snapshot. Its immutable Quest Story Codex retains each available description, objective, closing account, item request and objective label. Source-specific atoms carry field/offset provenance, stage gates, named roles, locative mentions, chain conditions and explicit speech uncertainty. Conditional promises remain conditional; source dialogue and reward offers never prove a player's actions or rewards. Missing fields remain unknown. Classic-derived rows are qualified reference material, not a guarantee that Forever has identical behavior. Unsupported branches and unavailable locales fail closed. This is not a claim of complete coverage of every quest in the game.

Full developer profiles and provenance reports are generated at build time. The runtime stores compact source strings once and decodes a bounded cache on demand; it does not copy the corpus into SavedVariables. Authored corrections are layered separately and retained when changed sources require review. Routes, spawn streams and unrelated expansions are excluded.

The build-time update pipeline discovers upstream releases, stages and validates source changes, preserves corrections, compiles all known quests and validates the full corpus at accepted/completed stages before its candidate gate. Failed network/freshness checks, removed IDs, schema/license changes or correction conflicts cannot produce a fresh public pass. An explicitly qualified pinned snapshot is permitted for private offline development. Users receive approved data in Warden updates. There is no external addon dependency, HTTP request or updater executing in the game.
