World-reference metadata attribution

The broad reference catalog includes factual NPC/world-layer metadata obtained
from ForeverDiff's public index. ForeverDiff attributes that layer to pfQuest
(vanilla) at commit 104f35678ca39ab1fb78b655f815cc7016f5e0c8, plus separately
contributed observations. It is not per-record native Forever placement proof.

Source: https://foreverdiff.gg/npcs
Provenance: https://foreverdiff.gg/about/data
Upstream: https://github.com/shagu/pfQuest
