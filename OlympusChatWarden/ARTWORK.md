# Artwork

`Media/IconScroll.tga` is the supplied, approved crest resized to a 256 × 256 RGBA
TGA. `Media/Bar.tga` provides the inline redaction texture. The interface references
game-supplied frames, parchment and controls; Blizzard texture files are not
redistributed.

The crest is an Olympus-inspired interpretation referencing the user's guild
addon: <https://github.com/dnl-gentile/olympus-addon>. Reference art belongs to its
owners. This community addon claims no endorsement from that project, guild
leadership, Asmongold or Blizzard.
