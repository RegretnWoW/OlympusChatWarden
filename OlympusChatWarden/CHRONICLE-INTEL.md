# Chronicle and Player Intelligence — 1.0.3

Choose whether to record under Settings > Chronicle & Intel. A first eligible
login offers Enable recording or Not now; reopen it with /ocw chronicle prompt.
Recording is independent of ordinary chat filtering and Chat Capture. Later
previews with valid scope5 consent keep that choice; older scopes need review.

Your own route is sampled every2 seconds while supported. Location samples retain
native precision and map identifiers; missing/secret coordinates stay absent.
At supported important local encounters, a single dated observer/subject position
snapshot may be saved. Other players are not continuously sampled. Historical
other-player tracks from earlier builds remain readable and backed up.

The existing collectors preserve supported progression, equipment/appearance,
quests, spells, inventory, reputation, achievements, native combat/credit/boss
records, scoreboard/meter objects, important-player/group evidence and permitted
Olympus public snapshots. API availability varies; names never substitute for a
missing GUID, and a public board entry is not a local sighting.

- Recorded Events: meaningful changes by default, with full-data and custom
  native action/background filters. Large searches yield in small steps.
- Activities: shared dungeon/raid/group and PvP visit segments, boss source
  evidence, dated roster associations and original-record navigation.
- Intelligence: measured time and UTC periods, personal encounter/credit history,
  roster relationships, PvP visits, unassigned scoreboards and progression/world
  summaries. The finished Chronicle/dossier visual experience remains future work.
- Collection Status: click any item or statistic for the saved fields and limits.
- Backup & Restore: complete-file backup instructions and optional text merging.

Recorded history stays on this computer until explicit Clear. Some incoming
facts already come from Olympus public information; ChatWarden does not upload
this history or automatically share it. A future sharing feature would need a
separate choice. No automatic age/count/size deletion is applied.

WoW writes SavedVariables on orderly logout/reload. With the game fully closed,
back up WTF/Account/<account>/SavedVariables/OlympusChatWarden.lua outside WoW.
The optional Windows tool creates one checksum-verified ZIP and keeps a safety
copy before restore. A full restore replaces settings and history; the advanced
text merge is history-only. Crashes may lose data not yet saved by the game.

Schema6 retains earlier formats and original imported-backup labels. Derived
summaries are in-memory references, not extra saved incident databases. The final
intelligence pass adds no collectors, getters, consent expansion or communication.
