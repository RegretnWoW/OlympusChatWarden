# Optional signed packs

Compatible with ChatWarden 1.0.1 and 1.0.2. The packs are unchanged for 1.0.2; current imports do not need replacing. Only Olympus
Protection v10 and King Protection v9 ship inside the addon. Optional packs
are separate manual imports.

**Start with the “Import These Packs” folder in the download ZIP.** Those files
use the current signed display names. See START-HERE.md for the name/version list.
The canonical files under `packs/` retain stable category IDs such as `gender`
and `slogans`; their signed contents are identical to the clearly named copies.
Updating the addon does not replace your saved imported packs. Import v11 to
replace older terminology titles; existing v11 imports need no reimport.

## Import or update

1. Choose a wanted `.ocwpack.txt` in “Import These Packs” and copy its entire string.
2. Open Packs > Import signed pack, paste, then Verify pack.
3. Review publisher/version and choose Install pack. Enable new categories only
   if wanted. Updates preserve existing enablement and sensitivity.

Older optional policy packs show Update Required and pause completely,
including their Gommage masks, until updated. Imports remain saved/exportable.
Body-label replacements v7 stays compatible without reimport. There is no
automatic download or installation. Historical decisions are not recomputed.

The six v11 terminology updates change only their signed titles and versions.
Compatible preview2 v10 imports stay active; import v11 to see the new names.
Pack IDs, vocabulary, policy capabilities, Gommage and saved choices are unchanged.
USA v6 and Labels v7 have unchanged models and payloads.

## Policy

Mild/Balanced/Strict use the same authored concepts. Neutral mentions, questions,
unendorsed reporting, criticism and uncertain interpretations stay visible.
Clear mild support gives Unchanged / Warning / Redacted; strong calls for
adoption or requirements give Warning / Redacted / Hidden. Gender and Inclusive
Language also cover deliberate own terminology practice. Gender covers explicit
pronoun declarations regardless of the values; Communism & Democratic Party covers
explicit own affiliation/voting for its selected concepts. These are opt-in
viewing preferences, not judgments about beliefs or misconduct.

USA Protection filters recognized attacks on its actual covered country/group;
praise, specific policy criticism and questions stay visible. Mild criticism,
including “America is dumb,” gives Unchanged / Warning / Redacted.

Gommage starts off and remains the separate literal meme option. All 215 masks
and 46 replacements are unchanged. Preferences, masks and replacements cannot
automatically Shield senders.
