# Authored policy contract 1

Schema 5 signs concepts and policy capabilities together. The existing OCWPACK1
envelope, author key and RSA-PSS/SHA-256 verification are unchanged. Only verified,
validated, compatible models enter compiled rule sets. No client vocabulary
override or post-verification substitution is used.

Mandatory policy object, with exactly these fields:

```json
{"contract":1,"mode":"support","declaration":"none","profile":"terminology"}
```

- Topic: mode `support` or `usage`, profile `terminology`, stance `avoid-support`.
  Rules are `topic` or `declaration`. Declaration rules require policy declaration
  `pronouns`; political self-affiliation uses policy declaration `affiliation`
  with signed topic references.
- Subject: mode `protection`, profile `country`, declaration `none`, stance
  `protect`. Rules are `reference` or `groupReference`.
- All schema-5 rules score 0. Context qualifies the expression; sensitivity
  selects its action. Statements and replacements are empty in schema 5.
- Gommage remains a separate signed literal list and adds no stance/abuse score.
- Labels remains schema 3, v7, with its exact 46 substitutions.

Generic bounded grammar is in ProtectionContext.lua and PreferenceContext.lua.
Optional vocabulary and capability choices are in signed models. Olympus/King
are the only included categories. Uncertain scope, stance or real-world meaning
stays unchanged. Independent qualified matches select the strongest action
without inflating severity from overlap. Optional terminology preference
matches always carry `shieldEligible=false`.

Display action, evidence and original target/expression offsets are separate.
Usage/declarations have explicit preference reasons; they are not labeled
harassment or inferred political beliefs. Reports are manual feedback and do
not modify models. History retains its original decision and timestamp.

Schemas 1–4 remain verifiable for preserved old imports, but old optional policy
packs are incompatible and inactive, including masks. Labels v7 is the exception.
Manual compatible signed updates preserve desired enablement and sensitivity.
Nothing is silently replaced or automatically fetched.
